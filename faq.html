FAQ

Clear answers to common questions

Before ordering — everything you need to know.

How is website pricing calculated?

Pages, bilingual support, portal, e-commerce, booking, content, SEO, hosting and support all affect pricing. Ready packages are available on the pricing page.

What exactly does the admin portal do?

Manage content, pages, prices, services, clients, tickets, social links and reports — no developer needed.

Is the website fully bilingual?

Yes — all copy, buttons, forms, messages and new pages are defined with Persian and English data and switch with one click.

Is there support after launch?

Yes — technical support, backups, security, content changes, SEO, campaigns and upgrades are available monthly.

Can an existing website be redesigned?

Yes — the current site is audited first, then structure, visuals, speed, copy and conversion path are all improved.

What is the delivery timeline?

Average delivery is 14 days. More complex projects with portal and e-commerce take 3 to 5 weeks.

Can live demos be shown to prospective clients?

Yes — salon, academy, clinic and shop demos have independent pages and features so clients have a real experience, not just screenshots.

Who owns the website?

100% yours. All files, domain, hosting, code and access credentials are handed over to you.
